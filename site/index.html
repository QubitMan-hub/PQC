Why now

Why this can't wait for the quantum computer

Nobody can break RSA or elliptic curves with a quantum computer yet. The problem is the traffic being recorded in the meantime, and the dates regulators have already set.

Today

Traffic captured now can be stored and decrypted once a large enough quantum computer exists. Anything that must stay secret for years is already at risk.

2030

NIST IR 8547 deprecates RSA and elliptic-curve keys at 112-bit security.

2035

Those algorithms are disallowed. Certificates you issue this year will still be in service.

Your certificate authority

Every identity, issued and revoked by you

An ML-DSA root, with its key encrypted in a file, in AWS KMS or in an HSM.

Issue

Server and client certificates signed with ML-DSA, or SLH-DSA roots for the longest-lived trust. Issuing CAs under an offline root.

Enrol automatically

Machines enrol and renew over EST (RFC 7030) with one-time tokens, or over ACME (RFC 8555); their keys never leave them.

Revoke

One command adds a certificate to the revocation list; edges fetch it every minute and refuse that certificate from the next connection.

Renew

pqc ca maintain, run daily, renews what expires within 30 days and re-signs the revocation list.

Try it

See it work in one minute

With Docker: a certificate authority, the edge in front of an ordinary web server, a post-quantum client that gets through and a classical one that is refused. Nothing is left behind.

docker build -t pqc https://github.com/QubitMan-hub/PQC.git#main
docker run --rm pqc

Or with Python 3.11+ and OpenSSL 3.5: pip install . in a clone, then pqc try. The step-by-step guide takes it from there.
